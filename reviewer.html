Participate as a Reviewer

Support the BSS 2026 programme committee by providing fair, constructive and timely reviews.

Join the review panel

BSS 2026 welcomes researchers and practitioners with relevant expertise in blockchain security, scalability, privacy, distributed systems and related areas.

To participate as a reviewer, write to muhammadajmal.azad@bcu.ac.uk. Include your name, affiliation, research areas, and a short summary of your reviewing or publication experience.

Reviewer responsibilities

  • Assess assigned papers against the workshop scope and review criteria.
  • Provide clear, constructive and confidential feedback to authors.
  • Declare conflicts of interest and complete reviews by the stated deadline.
